OUR STORY
The beginning · 2005

It began with a single clinic.

In 2005, the philanthropist Himayatullah Sadukhani opened a small clinic in Nazimabad with one urgent purpose — dignified healthcare for families who could not afford it.

Karachi's rapid expansion and deep inequality had left millions without a reliable safety net. That single clinic became the seed of a thriving, multi-sector institution.

Healthcare, education and clean water are fundamental human rights — independent of financial capacity.

SERVICES
The clinical ecosystem

Comprehensive care, under one roof.

A 24-hour facility serving hundreds of low-income patients daily — removing the need to travel between clinics, and the cost barriers that delay care.

24-Hour Urgent Care
Round-the-clock stabilisation for trauma, cardiac, respiratory & pediatric emergencies.
Multi-Specialty OPD
Senior consultants — pediatrics, gynecology, cardiology, gastroenterology & more.
Diagnostic Centre
Certified lab, digital X-ray, ECG & ultrasound for accurate clinical decisions.
In-House Pharmacy
Qualified pharmacists, strict inventory control, essential medicines always in stock.
Ambulance & Free Equipment
24-hour transport plus oxygen, wheelchairs & nebulisers — free for home use.
Clean Water (WASH)
Sabeel, deep bore wells & an RO plant certified by AKU & PCSIR laboratories.

THE FUTURE
Capital expansion

Building the next chapter.

Five planned wings will transform the Medical Centre into a comprehensive hospital — each a distinct capital opportunity for institutional and diaspora donors.

Neonatal ICU
Incubators, ventilators & phototherapy against high neonatal mortality.
Maternity & Gynecology
Safe 24-hour maternal care — 2 labour rooms & 4 operating theatres.
Eye Department & OT
Targets preventable blindness, cataracts & diabetic retinopathy.
Dental Clinic
Affordable oral care — dental chairs, digital X-ray & surgery suite.
Physiotherapy Centre
Rehabilitation for stroke, spinal & orthopedic-trauma recovery.
Sponsor a room, a machine, or a whole wing — every funded project carries a permanent dedication plaque, progress updates and annual patient-impact reports.
