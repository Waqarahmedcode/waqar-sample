STEP 01 The Beginning · 2005

It began with a single clinic.

In 2005, the philanthropist Himayatullah Sadukhani opened a small clinic in Nazimabad with one urgent purpose — dignified healthcare for families who could not afford it.

Karachi's rapid expansion and deep inequality had left millions without a reliable safety net. That single clinic became the seed of a thriving, multi-sector institution built on a simple belief: healthcare, education and clean water are fundamental human rights — independent of financial capacity.
